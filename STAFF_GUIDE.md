# Race-day staff guide

This guide is for volunteers at the registration desk. Keep it on the table.

## Before you leave home

1. Charge the phone or tablet you'll use. Bring a power bank.
2. Sign in to the series website with your staff email.
3. Open **Staff**, then **Open race-day check-in**. Wait until the screen shows "Roster downloaded."
4. Add the page to your home screen (in the browser's share or menu button, choose **Add to Home Screen**). It then opens like an app.

Opening check-in once with a signal saves the roster and the screen on the device. After that, check-in works with no signal at all.

## Checking in a rider

1. Tap **Scan a code** and point the camera at the code on the rider's phone. Or type their name or bib number in the search box and tap their name.
2. Look at the badges:
   - **Paid**: they paid online or at the desk.
   - **Waiver signed**: they signed this season's waiver.
   - **License on file**: they gave a USA Cycling license number.
   - **Check eligibility**: read the note. For example, a Masters rider who looks too young. Check their license or ID, type a short reason, and tap **Record override**.
3. Check the bib number. If the rider already has one, it's filled in. Otherwise, the screen suggests the next free number. Change it to match the bib you hand them.
4. Tap **Check in**, hand over the bib, and tap **Back to the list**.

If you checked in the wrong rider, open them and tap **Undo check-in**. Undo needs a signal.

## When something's missing

- **Not paid**: take payment, choose the method (cash, card at the desk, Stripe payment link, or comp), check the amount, and tap **Record payment**. Then check them in.
- **Waiver needed**: tap **Hand to rider to sign** and give them the device. The screen shows only the waiver. Riders under 18 need a parent or guardian to sign on the same screen. When it says "You're signed," take the device back and tap **Staff: return to check-in**.
- **Rider not on the list**: they aren't registered. Use **Walk-up**.

## Walk-up registration

Walk-ups need a signal. With no signal, write the rider's details on paper and enter them when the signal comes back.

1. Tap **Walk-up**.
2. Search for the rider by name or email. If they've raced before, tap their name.
3. If they're new, fill in their name, birth date, and an emergency contact. Email is optional but lets them see their results later.
4. Choose the category and payment method, check the amount, and tap **Register the rider**.
5. If they haven't signed the waiver, hand them the device to sign. Then check them in.

## No signal

A red banner says "Offline" and counts the changes waiting to send. Keep working: every check-in, payment, and waiver is saved on the device. When the signal returns, the changes send on their own and the banner goes away.

- Don't clear the browser or sign out while changes are waiting.
- If two volunteers check in the same rider while offline, the app keeps the earliest check-in.
- If a change can't be saved (for example, two riders given the same bib), a red box explains why. Fix it on the rider's screen, then tap **Dismiss**.
- If the banner says your sign-in expired, open a new tab, sign in again, and come back. Your changes are still saved.

## Start lists for the officials

At the bottom of the check-in screen, tap **Start list (PDF)** to print, or **Start list (CSV)** for a spreadsheet. Both list confirmed riders by category and bib, with a column showing who's checked in. Start lists need a signal.

## Weather and course notes

To warn riders about the course (for example, "Course is wet in turn 3"), open **Staff**, then **Event information**, and pick the event. Type the note under **Weather and course note** and tap **Save**. It shows above the forecast on the event page.

## Posting an announcement

1. Open **Staff**, then **Announcements**.
2. Type a title and, if needed, a short message.
3. Pick the event it's about, or leave it as the whole series.
4. Choose **Urgent** for anything riders must see before they arrive, such as a course change or a delayed start. Urgent announcements show as a red banner on the home page.
5. To email it, choose **Riders registered for the event** or **Everyone registered this season**. Emails go out once, when the announcement is posted.
6. Tap **Post announcement**.

To take an announcement down, tap **Remove** next to it.

## Posting results

1. Get the officials' results as a CSV file, one category at a time. A spreadsheet works too: copy the rows, including the header row.
2. Open **Staff**, then **Results**, and pick the race and category.
3. Choose the file, or paste the rows, and tap **Preview results**. If the sheet has a problem, such as two riders in the same place, the app lists the lines to fix.
4. Check each match. Riders are matched by bib, then by name. Fix any wrong match with the menu under the row. Riders who aren't registered stay "Not matched": they show in the race results but don't score series points.
5. Tap **Save results as a draft**. Nothing is public yet.
6. Check the saved list and the points, then tap **Publish results**. The results appear on the event page, and the standings update.

To correct published results, upload the fixed sheet. Saving takes the race off the public pages until you publish again. **Unpublish results** hides a race without changing it.
